# 과업 동사의 교범 정의

한국어 과업 동사 32개 가운데 미 육군 교범에 대응 정의가 있는 27개와 예비의 정의다. 한 동사에 조항이 둘인 것도 있다. 파이프라인은 이 목록을 그대로 프롬프트에 넣는다(조건 D, DF).
대응 조항을 고르지 않은 동사: 정찰·격퇴·견제·공격·초월 (교범 용어와 1:1로 맞지 않음).

| 동사 | 교범 용어 | 조항 | 정의(첫 문장) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 탈취 | seize | FM 3-90 B-57 | Seize is a tactical mission task in which a unit takes possession of a designated area by using overwhelming force. |
| 확보 | secure | FM 3-90 B-56 | Secure is a tactical mission task in which a unit prevents the enemy from damaging or destroying a force, facility, or geographical location. |
| 점령 | occupy | FM 3-90 B-53 | Occupy is a tactical mission task in which a unit moves into an area to control it without enemy opposition. |
| 고수 | retain | FM 3-90 B-55 | Retain is a tactical mission task in which a unit prevents enemy occupation or use of terrain. |
| 소탕 | clear | FM 3-90 B-16 | Clear is a tactical mission task in which a unit eliminates all enemy forces within an assigned area. |
| 격멸 | destroy | FM 3-90 B-23 | Destroy is a tactical mission task that physically renders an enemy force combat-ineffective until it is reconstituted. |
| 와해 | disrupt | FM 3-90 B-29 | Disrupt is a tactical mission task in which a unit upsets an enemy’s formation or tempo and causes the enemy force to attack prematurely or in a piecemeal fashion. |
| 고착 | fix | FM 3-90 B-35 | Fix is a tactical mission task in which a unit prevents the enemy from moving from a specific location for a specific period. |
| 차단 | block / interdict | FM 3-90 B-5 | Block is a tactical mission task that denies the enemy access to an area or an avenue of approach. |
| 차단 | interdict | FM 3-90 B-46 | Interdict is a tactical mission task where a unit prevents, disrupts, or delays the enemy’s use of an area or route in any domain. |
| 저지 | contain | FM 3-90 B-19 | Contain is a tactical mission task in which a unit stops, holds, or surrounds an enemy force. |
| 봉쇄 | isolate | FM 3-90 B-51 | Isolate is a tactical mission task in which a unit seals off an enemy, physically and psychologically, from sources of support and denies it freedom of movement. |
| 무력화 | neutralize | FM 3-90 B-52 | Neutralize is a tactical mission task in which a unit renders the enemy incapable of interfering with an operation. |
| 돌파 | breach | FM 3-90 B-7 | Breach is a tactical mission task in which a unit breaks through or establishes a passage through an enemy obstacle. |
| 우회 | bypass | FM 3-90 B-9 | Bypass is a tactical mission task in which a unit deliberately avoids contact with an obstacle or an enemy force. |
| 역습 | counterattack | FM 3-90 5-124 | A counterattack is a variation of attack by a defending force against an attacking enemy force. |
| 방어 | area defense | FM 3-90 8-8 | An area defense is a type of defensive operation that concentrates on denying enemy forces access to designated terrain for a specific time rather than destroying the enemy outright (ADP 3-90). |
| 방어 | area defense 편성 | FM 3-90 9-4 | An area defense is organized with a security force, main body, and a reserve. |
| 지연 | delay | FM 3-90 11-13 | A delay is when a force under pressure trades space for time by slowing down the enemy’s momentum and inflicting maximum damage on enemy forces without becoming decisively engaged (ADP 3-90). |
| 지연 | delay | FM 3-90 11-16 | A delay succeeds by causing enemy forces to fight through a series of defensive positions. |
| 추격 | pursuit | FM 3-90 7-1 | A pursuit is a type of offensive operation to catch or cut off a disorganized hostile force attempting to escape, with the aim of destroying it. |
| 철수 | withdrawal | FM 3-90 11-81 | A withdrawal may be assisted or unassisted. |
| 이탈 | disengage | FM 3-90 B-24 | Disengage is a tactical mission task in which a unit breaks contact with an enemy to conduct another mission or to avoid becoming decisively engaged. |
| 엄호 | cover | FM 3-90 13-99 | Cover is a type of security operation done independent of the main body to protect them by fighting to gain time while preventing enemy ground observation of and direct fire against the main body (ADP 3- 90). |
| 경계 | guard | FM 3-90 13-72 | Guard is a type of security operation conducted to protect the main body by fighting to gain time while preventing enemy ground observation of and direct fire against the main body (ADP 3-90). |
| 차장 | screen | FM 3-90 13-56 | Screen is a type of security operation that primarily provides early warning to the protected force (ADP 3-90). |
| 후속지원 | follow and support | FM 3-90 B-42 | Follow and support is a tactical mission task in which a committed force follows and supports a lead force conducting an offensive operation. |
| 후속인수 | follow and assume | FM 3-90 B-38 | Follow and assume is a tactical mission task in which a committed force follows and supports a lead force conducting an offensive operation and continues the mission if the lead force cannot continue. |
| 화력지원 | support by fire | FM 3-90 B-58 | Support by fire is a tactical mission task in which a unit engages the enemy by direct fire in support of another maneuvering force. |
| 화력공격 | attack by fire | FM 3-90 B-3 | Attack by fire is a tactical mission task using direct and indirect fires to engage an enemy from a distance. |
| 예비 | reserve | FM 3-90 9-71 | The reserve may be a designated unit or a provisional force organized from available personnel and equipment. |
| 역습 | counterattack force | FM 3-90 5-130 | A counterattack force is a committed force if its commander’s defensive scheme hinges on a counterattack to destroy, dislocate, disintegrate, or isolate the attacking enemy force. |

출처: FM 3-90 Tactics (May 2023)
